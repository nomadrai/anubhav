# Behaviour, protection and doubts — agent QA

69 original pairs: behaviour 16, fraud 22, doubts 31. Each English/Hindi pair was compared during authoring for meaning, negation, agency, uncertainty and scope. Hindi keeps short spoken sentences and does not strengthen a possibility into certainty. Status remains agent-checked; no native-speaker/listening review or legal approval is claimed. App and basics/products evidence are in APP_COVERAGE.md and BASICS_PRODUCTS_QA.md. Final file hashes include the integration-added, entry-specific lintAllow records; those records change lint metadata, not the explanations.

Semantic back-translation checks: FOMO = someone else's reported gain creates pressure, not evidence of your gain; herd = popularity is not proof; panic = fear can narrow attention, but not every exit is panic; revenge = another trade does not owe recovery; overconfidence = one gain cannot separate ability/chance/conditions; hindsight = knowing the ending changes remembered uncertainty. Borrowing = losses do not erase repayment obligations; instant credit = speed does not establish total cost; emergency savings = unexpected needs and timing, not a prescribed amount/product. Bias/anchoring/control/social-proof/pause entries retain the same non-diagnostic limits.

Fraud pairs retain possibility rather than accusation: claims/screenshots/registration numbers are not independent evidence; authentic registration does not guarantee an outcome. Fixed payment and guaranteed market return are different ideas. Pump-and-dump and Ponzi entries describe mechanisms without accusing an entity. Remote access/OTP/password/phishing entries describe access risks, never request credentials. Complaint/reporting entries distinguish reporting from compensation. Recovery scams and victim-blame entries neither promise restitution nor blame a person. Loss alone does not establish fraud, and gain alone does not establish honesty.

Doubts are answered directly and in balance: no real deposit/cash reward/advice/brokerage/certification/regulator endorsement; publisher identity is not invented. No unsupported trader-loss percentage is stated. A friend's gain can be genuine without predicting another person's outcome. Markets are not reduced to gambling; good leverage outcomes and small-investor possibilities are not denied. History is an observation, not a forecast; the learning account is a calculation. AI/library limits, weak-network/cache conditions, Hindi review limits and the distinction between practice answers and explicitly sent chat questions are retained in both languages.
